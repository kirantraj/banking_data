SECTION 2 — DATA PRIVACY & GDPR/CCPA COMPLIANCE

Data Handling Policy (Version 3.2 — Effective 2024-01-01):
  • Customer PII is stored only in approved GCP regions (us-central1).
  • BigQuery datasets are encrypted at rest using customer-managed
    encryption keys (CMEK) via Cloud KMS.
  • Data retention: transaction records retained for 7 years per BSA.
  • Right-to-erasure requests: 2 received in Q1 2024; both fulfilled
    within the 30-day regulatory window.
  • Data access logs reviewed monthly; no unauthorised access detected.

AI & Analytics Governance:
  The Banking Data Copilot system (AI agent + BigQuery integration)
  operates under the following controls:
    1. SQL query validation — no DML operations permitted.
    2. Row-level access controls enforced at the BigQuery layer.
    3. All AI-generated queries logged with full audit trail.
    4. LLM prompts do not include raw PII — customer IDs used instead.
    5. Results classified as INTERNAL USE ONLY.

SECTION 3 — PRODUCT COMPLIANCE

Savings Account Regulation (Regulation D):
  As of 2020, Regulation D limits on monthly withdrawals have been
  lifted by the Federal Reserve; however, the bank maintains an
  internal soft limit of 6 free withdrawals per month to manage
  liquidity risk.  No violations in Q1 2024.

Credit Card Regulation (CARD Act 2009):
  All credit card products comply with the Credit CARD Act requirements:
    • 21-day billing cycle minimum — met.
    • Rate increase notices (45-day advance) — met.
    • Over-limit fees: NOT charged (bank policy).
  Complaint escalations in Q1: 4 (all resolved within 5 business days).

Home & Personal Loan Compliance (TILA / Regulation Z):
  All loan disclosures include:
    • Annual Percentage Rate (APR)
    • Finance charge
    • Amount financed
    • Total of payments
  No TILA violations identified in Q1 2024.
