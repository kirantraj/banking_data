CASE FC-2024-001 | HIGH PRIORITY
Date: 2024-01-19
Customer: C005 | Region: North
Product: Credit Card
Transaction Amount: $12,000.75
Type: Debit

Summary:
Customer C005 initiated an unusually large credit card debit transaction
of $12,000.75 — approximately 4.2× their 90-day average transaction value.
The transaction originated from an unrecognised IP address in a foreign
country while the customer's physical card was reported in use domestically
at the same timestamp. Suspected card-not-present fraud.

Actions Taken:
  • Card blocked within 2 hours of detection by automated monitoring system.
  • Customer notified via SMS and email.
  • Chargeback process initiated with the merchant.
  • Case forwarded to Financial Crimes Unit for further investigation.
  • Provisional credit of $12,000.75 applied to customer account pending review.

Status: UNDER INVESTIGATION

CASE FC-2024-002 | MEDIUM PRIORITY
Date: 2024-01-22
Customer: C008 | Region: West
Product: Credit Card
Transaction Amount: $4,500.25
Type: Debit

Summary:
Three successive debit transactions of similar amounts ($4,500.25, $4,490.00,
$4,510.75) were attempted within a 6-minute window on 2024-01-22, triggering
the velocity-check rule in the fraud detection engine.
Pattern is consistent with card testing — fraudsters make multiple similar
amounts to verify card validity before a larger purchase.

Actions Taken:
  • Velocity rule triggered automatic transaction hold after second attempt.
  • Two of three transactions declined; first transaction approved before rule fired.
  • Customer contacted for verification — confirmed card lost two days prior.
  • Replacement card issued; dispute process initiated for the approved transaction.

Status: RESOLVED — REFUND ISSUED

CASE FC-2024-003 | CRITICAL
Date: 2024-02-02
Customer: C018 | Region: South
Product: Credit Card
Transaction Amount: $15,600.00
Type: Debit

Summary:
Customer C018 contacted the bank on 2024-02-02 to dispute a $15,600.00
debit transaction. Customer states no knowledge of this transaction.
Internal investigation revealed the customer's online banking credentials
were compromised via a phishing email sent 72 hours prior to the transaction.
The funds were transferred to a mule account before the fraud was identified.

Actions Taken:
  • Online banking access suspended immediately upon fraud report.
  • Credential reset enforced; multi-factor authentication enabled.
  • Law enforcement notified per regulatory obligation (AML reporting threshold exceeded).
  • Mule account flagged with correspondent bank; fund recovery attempt in progress.
  • Customer compensated in full per the bank's fraud liability policy.

Status: LAW ENFORCEMENT REFERRAL — FUND RECOVERY PENDING
